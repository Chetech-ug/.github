# CheTech UG

**CheTech UG** builds simple, practical health-tech tools to solve real problems faced by people in Uganda.

## Why we exist
Many communities still face long waits for care, lost patient records, limited access to reliable health information, and services that are hard to reach. Those problems cause stress, delayed treatment, and extra costs for families.

We believe technology should make healthcare easier, safer, and more human.

## What we do
- Build user-friendly web and mobile tools that help patients and health workers.
- Use AI to provide smart guidance, triage help, and data-driven insights.
- Explore blockchain to protect patient records and make them tamper proof and private.
- Work closely with health workers, organizations, and communities to build solutions that actually help.

## Our approach
1. Start small and practical. Deliver working features that solve clear problems.
2. Keep people at the center. Design for users with limited bandwidth, intermittent power, and low-cost devices.
3. Use modern tech thoughtfully. AI and blockchain are tools, not the goal. We use them where they add clear value.
4. Collaborate openly. We welcome partners, contributors, and feedback.
